Academic Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

This portfolio showcases data visualization and business intelligence projects developed during my academic coursework. Each project demonstrates core data engineering skills, including data modeling (Star schema), DAX measures, interactive drill-downs, dynamic filters, and clean visual storytelling designed for academic decision-makers.
